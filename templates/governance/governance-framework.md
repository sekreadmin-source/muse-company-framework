## 1. Structure & roles

- **Owner** — the human. Holds final authority.
- **COO agent** — orchestration: monitoring, commanding, verification,
  unblocking. Owns no desk work.
- **Division leads** — own their division's board, schedule, and output quality.
- **Researchers** — resource unblockers: first movers when a tool, platform,
  or data source fails; keepers of the division's resource map.
- **Worker agents (optional)** — external executors under work packages.

## 2. Decision classes

Define yours. A workable default split:

| Class | Decided by |
| --- | --- |
| Funds movement, spending above threshold | Owner |
| Identity: registrations, KYC, final submissions | Owner |
| Major choices: new lines of business, partnerships | Owner |
| Routine execution inside a division's mandate | Division lead |
| Operational unblocking, scheduling, sequencing | COO |
| Findings verification before owner reporting | COO / lead |
